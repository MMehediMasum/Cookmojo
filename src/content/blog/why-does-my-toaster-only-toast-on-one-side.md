### The Sneaky Bagel Button



Look at your machine right now. Does it have a button for bagels? Most new ones do. This button is very sneaky.



When you press it, it changes how the machine works. It only turns on the heat coils in the middle slots.



* **The cut side** gets hot and crispy.
* **The round back** of the bagel stays soft.



I saw the little blue light on my bagel button shining bright. It was mocking me! I pressed the button to turn it off. I put in a new slice of bread. Boom. I got perfect toast. Try your bread again before you buy a new machine.



### What Is Bread Toasted on One Side Called?



Here is a fun fact. Some people actually want their bread like this. If you wonder what is bread toasted on one side called, chefs have a name for it. They call it "half-toast" or "single-sided toast."



The toast done on one side meaning actually comes from old English pubs. Cooks there use it to make perfect cheese on toast. Here is how they do it:



* You toast just one side of the bread until it is hard.
* You take it out of the slots.
* You put thick cheese on the hot, toasted side.
* You put the whole thing under a hot broiler in the oven.



The bottom stays soft, so it does not burn or get hard like a rock. The top gets gooey and perfect. It tastes amazing.

| **Bagel Mode** | Only the inner walls heat up | Bagels, thick buns, English muffins |
| **Half-Toast** | One side gets heat, one side stays cool | Cheese on toast, old pub snacks |

### A Broken Heating Element



Look down into the dark slots. Do you see those thin metal wires? Those are the heating coils. Here is what I do to check them.



Push the lever down. Do not put bread in yet. Just look inside. You should see a bright red glow. You will feel warm air hit your face. It is like a tiny campfire in your kitchen.



But what if one wall stays dark? If you see no red glow on one side, that wire is dead. Over time, these wires get old. They get very hot, they cool down, and one day, they snap. I saw this in my old silver machine. A cut wire means that side stays totally cold.



### The Great Crumb Mountain



I have a confession. I rarely clean my crumb tray. We are all guilty of this! Think about it. When did you last empty yours?



Old bits of food pile up fast. They form a thick, black mountain at the bottom. These old crumbs cause big problems.



* They block the heat from reaching your food.
* They jam the moving parts inside the slots.
* They make a really bad smell.



One morning, my whole house smelled like burnt ash. It was awful. The smoke actually made my eyes water. It turned out, a giant chunk of burnt crust was blocking the heat coils on one side.





Here is a quick look at what might be wrong inside your machine:

## How to Fix a Toaster Not Working on One Side

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=last-sunday-morning,-i-stood-in-my-messy-kitchen-in-seattle.-i-was-so-hungry.&w=424&h=324&c=7)



Last Sunday morning, I stood in my messy kitchen in Seattle. I was so hungry. I stared at my sad bread. I asked the air, why does my [toaster only toast on one side](https://cookmojo.com/why-is-my-toaster-smoking/)? I refused to eat cold bread again. I decided to fix it right then.



Grab a tall trash can. First, unplug the thick cord from the wall right now. Safety comes first. Do not skip this step. Now, let us try to save your breakfast.



### Step 1: The Shake Down



Pull out that thin metal tray at the very bottom. Dump those black, smelly crumbs into the trash. Next, take the machine over to the sink.



Turn the whole thing upside down. Shake it hard. Give the bottom a few firm smacks with your hand. You will hear a funny rattle inside.



Watch out! A rain of old sesame seeds and hard crusts will fall. They make a loud ping when they hit the sink. Sometimes, a giant chunk of hard food blocks the heat wire. This fast, messy trick can actually clear the jam and fix it.



### Step 2: The Flashlight Test



If the shake down fails, try this next. Grab your phone and turn on the bright flashlight. Shine that light deep inside the dark slots.



Look very closely at the inner walls. Do you see a snapped wire? They look like tiny metal ribbons. If a wire is cut or hanging loose, you are sadly out of luck.



Do not try to tie it back together. Fixing a broken toaster wire is very hard. Worse, it is a huge fire hazard. The risk is just not worth a hot meal. If that wire is dead, it is time to buy a new machine.




| **Fixing Step** | **Tools You Need** | **What You Are Doing** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The Shake Down** | Trash can, kitchen sink | Dropping out large chunks of burnt food |
| **The Flashlight Test** | Phone light, your eyes | Looking for a dead or snapped heat wire |
| **The Final Call** | Your wallet | Buying a safe, new machine if wires are cut |

### Frequently Asked Questions



**Can old crumbs stop my toaster from working?**



Yes, they can do that. Big chunks of burnt food pile up fast inside. They block the hot wire. This keeps the heat from your bread. Clean the tray to make the heat flow!



**Is it safe to use a toaster with a broken heating element?**



No, it is not safe at all. A snapped heat wire is a bad fire risk. Do not try to tie the wire back up. Unplug the broken machine. Go buy a new one to stay safe today.



**Can burnt food ruin the metal coils in my machine?**



Yes, thick burnt bits can hurt the heat coils over time. If you wonder why does my toaster only [toast on one side](https://cookmojo.com/why-does-my-toaster-keep-tripping-the-electricity/), check for old food. Clean the tray right now!



**Is it safe to eat bread that is hot on top but cold below?**



Yes, it is safe to eat half hot bread. It is just a fun texture. You get a good crunch on top and soft dough on the back. Try it with thick peanut butter today.



**Will a dead wire cause a fire in my kitchen?**



A snapped wire is a big fire risk. Do not plug it in. Do not try to tie the loose ends. It is best to throw the bad machine out. Go buy a new and safe one now.



**Can I replace a snapped heat band by myself at home?**



No, fixing a broken heat coil is hard and bad to do. It can spark and start a big fire. Do not take that risk for a hot meal. Get a fresh machine from a store.



**How often should I empty the bottom tray for best results?**



You should clean the crumb tray once a week. This keeps the hot air free to flow. It stops bad ash smells, too. A clean tool makes the best warm morning meals.
