# About Us



Welcome to **CookMojo**, your trusted destination for honest kitchen gear reviews, practical cooking tips, and culinary inspiration. We believe that cooking should never feel like a chore. With the right tools and a little bit of guidance, anyone can transform their kitchen into a space of creativity, efficiency, and joy.



Whether you are outfitting your very first apartment kitchen or upgrading to professional-grade appliances, we are here to help you make the best possible decisions for your culinary needs.

## Meet Our Expert: Sheryll Berrett





Behind every great recommendation at CookMojo is **Sheryll Berrett**, our lead food writer and head gear tester.



Sheryll spent eight years grinding it out in busy, fast-paced local kitchens. She knows firsthand what separates a marketing gimmick from a truly essential kitchen tool. Now, she has traded the commercial kitchen chaos to share her top cooking tips and hands-on reviews directly with you.



She rigorously tests every pot, pan, and chef's knife so you never have to guess what actually works before you buy. Her ultimate goal is simple: to make home cooking fun, fast, and incredibly easy for cooks of all skill levels. When she is not putting the latest high-speed blender through its paces, you can usually find her dialing in her home barista skills and brewing the perfect shot of espresso.

## What We Do

At CookMojo, we take the guesswork out of shopping for kitchen supplies. Our approach is straightforward and user-focused:



* **Hands-On Testing:** We chop, blend, sear, and bake using the equipment we recommend to ensure it holds up to real-world use.
* **Curated Top Picks:** We sift through thousands of products to highlight only the best, most durable, and most cost-effective items on the market.
* **Actionable Advice:** Alongside our reviews, we provide practical, everyday cooking tips to help you get the absolute most out of your new gear.



### Our Commitment to Transparency



Trust is the most important ingredient in our kitchen. CookMojo is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program. This means that when you click on our links and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission at no additional cost to you.



This helps keep the lights on and the test kitchen running, but it never influences our reviews. We maintain strict editorial independence—if a product does not pass Sheryll's stress tests, it does not make the cut on our site.
